📖 What is this tool?

This software helps you prepare documents for artificial intelligence systems. It takes files like PDFs, Word documents, PowerPoint presentations, and HTML pages and turns them into clean text. This process is essential if you want to build a RAG pipeline, which is a method that allows AI to answer questions based on your specific files.

The tool saves these processed files directly into your Backblaze B2 cloud storage. It handles the difficult work of breaking large documents into small, manageable pieces that computers can understand. You do not need expensive paid keys to run this, as the software uses models that work directly on your own computer.

⚙️ System Requirements

  • Operating System: Windows 10 or 11.
  • Processor: Modern multi-core CPU (Intel i5 or AMD Ryzen 5 recommended).
  • Memory: At least 8GB of RAM.
  • Storage: 2GB of available disk space.
  • Network: A reliable internet connection for cloud storage synchronization.

🛠️ Using the Pipeline

Once you open the application, you will see a simple control panel. To process your documents, follow these steps:

Step 1: Configure Storage
Enter your Backblaze B2 credentials. This allows the program to save your processed data to your private cloud storage. The software connects using an S3-compatible method, which is standard for cloud storage services.

Step 2: Add Files
Click the "Add Files" button to select the documents you want to process. You can select individual files or entire folders containing your documents.

Step 3: Process Documents
Click the "Start" button. The software scans your files, converts the formatting, and breaks the text into segments. These segments are optimized for AI systems. You can monitor the progress bar as the software works.

Step 4: Verify Results
After the process ends, log into your Backblaze B2 account. You will find your clean Markdown files stored in the bucket you selected during the setup phase.

🔒 Privacy and Data

Your data stays under your control. Because this software uses models that run on your computer, your documents are not sent to external servers for processing. The only data transfer occurs between your computer and your personal Backblaze B2 bucket.

💡 Frequently Asked Questions

Does this require an internet connection?
Yes, you need a connection to sync the finished files with your Backblaze B2 storage. The processing itself happens offline.

Can I process multiple file types at once?
Yes, the software automatically detects the file type and applies the correct conversion method for PDFs, Word files, and others.
